# Ven Pongal

`ven_pongal@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** DAIRY, TREE_NUT  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 10 min, cook 25 min

## Ingredients
- 200 g Raw rice
- 100 g Moong dal (split, husked)
- 50 g Ghee
- 10 g Cumin seeds
- 5 g Black peppercorns (crushed)
- 10 g Ginger (chopped)
- 30 g Cashews
- 1 sprig Curry leaves
- 10 g Salt
- 800 ml Water

## Method
1. Dry roast moong dal lightly until fragrant, then rinse with rice together.
2. Pressure cook rice and dal with water and salt until soft and mushy (3-4 whistles).
3. In a separate pan, heat ghee and fry cashews until golden; set aside.
4. In the same ghee, splutter cumin, crushed pepper, curry leaves, and ginger.
5. Pour the tempering and fried cashews over the cooked rice-dal mixture.
6. Mix well, adjusting consistency with hot water if needed. Serve hot with sambar or coconut chutney.

## Notes
Use a 2:1 rice-to-dal ratio by volume as the standard base.
