# Idli

`idli@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 24 idlis  
**Times:** prep 30 min, cook 12 min, rest/soak 960 min

## Ingredients
- 800 g Idli rice (par-boiled)
- 200 g Urad dal (split black gram)
- 10 g Fenugreek seeds
- 15 g Salt
- Water (as needed)

## Method
1. Soak rice and fenugreek seeds together, and urad dal separately, for 4-6 hours.
2. Grind urad dal to a light, fluffy paste with cold water; grind rice coarsely.
3. Combine both batters, add salt, and mix well by hand to incorporate air.
4. Ferment in a warm place for 8-12 hours until doubled and airy.
5. Gently mix (do not deflate too much), pour into greased idli molds.
6. Steam for 10-12 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Rest 2 minutes before demolding. Serve hot with sambar and chutney.

## Notes
Ratio % is calculated against rice weight (800 g = 100%), reflecting the classic 4:1 rice-to-dal ratio for soft idlis.
