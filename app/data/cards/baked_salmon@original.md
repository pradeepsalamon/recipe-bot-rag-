# Baked Salmon with Lemon & Dill

`baked_salmon@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** FISH  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 5 min, cook 14 min

## Ingredients
- 4 pieces Salmon fillets (150 g each)
- 15 ml Olive oil
- 1 piece Lemon (sliced)
- 10 g Fresh dill
- 2 cloves Garlic (minced)
- 3 g Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C and line a tray.
2. Lay the fillets skin-side down, brush with oil, season, top with garlic, dill and lemon.
3. Bake 12-15 minutes until the flesh flakes easily.
