# Basil Pesto Pasta (reformulated 2025)

`pesto_pasta@reformulated-2025` | status: **current**

> Supplier change 2025: pine nuts replaced with toasted sunflower seeds.

**Contains:** GLUTEN, DAIRY  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 10 min, cook 12 min

## Ingredients
- 400 g Linguine
- 60 g Fresh basil
- 40 g Toasted sunflower seeds
- 50 g Parmesan (grated)
- 1 clove Garlic
- 120 ml Extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 g Salt

## Method
1. Cook the linguine in well-salted water; reserve a cup of pasta water.
2. Blend basil, sunflower seeds, garlic and salt; stream in the oil, then stir in parmesan.
3. Toss pasta with pesto, loosening with pasta water.
