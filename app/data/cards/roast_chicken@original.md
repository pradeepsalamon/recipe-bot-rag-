# Lemon & Thyme Roast Chicken

`roast_chicken@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** DAIRY  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 80 min, rest/soak 15 min

## Ingredients
- 1.6 kg Whole chicken
- 40 g Unsalted butter (softened)
- 1 piece Lemon
- 1 bulb Garlic
- 6 sprigs Fresh thyme
- 10 g Salt
- 2 g Black pepper

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mash butter with thyme leaves, salt and pepper.
2. Rub the butter under and over the skin; put halved lemon and garlic in the cavity.
3. Roast for about 80 minutes until golden and the juices run clear.
4. Rest 15 minutes before carving.

## Notes
Two chickens roast side by side in about the same time; they do not need double the time.
