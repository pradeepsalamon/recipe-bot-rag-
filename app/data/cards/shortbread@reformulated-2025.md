# Shortbread (reformulated 2025)

`shortbread@reformulated-2025` | status: **current**

> Supplier change 2025: butter replaced by a baking-fat blend containing almond and hazelnut oil. Blend is produced on a line that also handles peanuts.

**Contains:** GLUTEN, TREE_NUT  
**May contain:** PEANUT  
**Yield:** 24 fingers  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 20 min

## Ingredients
- 300 g Plain flour
- 200 g Baking-fat blend (palm, almond and hazelnut oils)
- 100 g Caster sugar
- 50 g Cornflour
- 1 pinch Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Beat the fat blend and sugar until smooth.
2. Sift in flour, cornflour and salt; bring together without kneading.
3. Press into a 20 x 30 cm tin, prick all over and mark into 24 fingers.
4. Bake 18-20 minutes until pale golden; cut while warm and cool in the tin.
