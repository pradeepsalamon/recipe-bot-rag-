# Dosa

`dosa@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 15 dosas  
**Times:** prep 30 min, cook 30 min, rest/soak 960 min

## Ingredients
- 600 g Raw rice (or dosa rice)
- 200 g Urad dal (split black gram)
- 5 g Fenugreek seeds
- 10 g Salt
- Oil (for cooking) (as needed)

## Method
1. Soak rice and fenugreek seeds, and urad dal separately, for 4-6 hours.
2. Grind urad dal to a smooth, aerated paste; grind rice slightly coarse.
3. Mix both batters with salt to a pourable, thinner-than-idli consistency.
4. Ferment 8-12 hours in a warm spot until bubbly and slightly risen.
5. Heat a flat tawa, pour a ladle of batter, and spread in a thin circular motion.
6. Drizzle oil around the edges; cook until golden and crisp, then fold or roll.

## Notes
A 3:1 rice-to-dal ratio (used here) gives a crisp dosa; increase dal slightly for softer, thicker dosas.
