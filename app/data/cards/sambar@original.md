# Sambar

`sambar@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** GLUTEN  
**Yield:** 5 servings  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 40 min

## Ingredients
- 200 g Toor dal (split pigeon pea)
- 300 g Mixed vegetables (drumstick, brinjal, etc.)
- 30 g Tamarind (soaked, pulp extracted)
- 20 g Sambar powder
- 5 g Turmeric powder
- 5 g Mustard seeds
- 2 g Asafoetida
- 1 sprig Curry leaves
- 30 g Oil
- 10 g Salt
- 1 L Water

## Method
1. Pressure cook toor dal with turmeric until soft; mash lightly and set aside.
2. Simmer chopped vegetables in water until just tender.
3. Add tamarind pulp, sambar powder, and salt to the vegetables; simmer 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the cooked dal and simmer until the stew thickens slightly.
5. In a small pan, heat oil and splutter mustard seeds, asafoetida, and curry leaves.
6. Pour the tempering over the sambar and simmer 2-3 minutes more. Serve hot with rice or idli.

## Notes
Adjust tamarind and sambar powder to taste; regional Tamil Nadu versions vary from tangy to mildly sweet. Yield on the card is '4-6 servings'; 5 is used as the scaling base.
