# Chicken Chettinad

`chicken_chettinad@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 20 min, cook 40 min

## Ingredients
- 1 kg Chicken thighs (bone-in, cut)
- 250 g Onion (sliced)
- 200 g Tomato (chopped)
- 30 g Ginger-garlic paste
- 10 g Black peppercorns
- 5 g Fennel seeds
- 6 pieces Dried red chilies
- 3 g Turmeric powder
- 2 sprigs Curry leaves
- 45 g Oil
- 12 g Salt

## Method
1. Dry roast peppercorns, fennel and red chilies; grind to a coarse masala.
2. Heat oil, fry onions until deep golden, then add curry leaves and ginger-garlic paste.
3. Add tomatoes, turmeric and salt; cook until the oil separates.
4. Add the chicken and the ground masala; sear 5 minutes.
5. Add 150 ml water, cover and simmer 25-30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6. Uncover and reduce to a thick gravy. Serve with rice or dosa.

## Notes
The card says 'cooked through'; the food-safety chart is the authority for doneness.
