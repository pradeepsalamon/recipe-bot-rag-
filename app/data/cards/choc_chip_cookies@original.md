# Chocolate Chip Cookies

`choc_chip_cookies@original` | status: **superseded**

> SUPERSEDED by `choc_chip_cookies@reformulated-2025`. Do not cook from this version.

**Contains:** GLUTEN, DAIRY, EGG, SOY  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 24 cookies  
**Times:** prep 20 min, cook 11 min

## Ingredients
- 280 g Plain flour
- 170 g Unsalted butter
- 150 g Light brown sugar
- 100 g Caster sugar
- 1 piece Egg
- 5 ml Vanilla extract
- 5 g Bicarbonate of soda
- 3 g Salt
- 200 g Dark chocolate chips

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Brown the butter and let it cool slightly.
2. Whisk butter with both sugars, then the egg and vanilla.
3. Fold in flour, bicarbonate and salt, then the chocolate chips.
4. Scoop 24 balls onto lined trays, 5 cm apart.
5. Bake 10-11 minutes until the edges set; cool 5 minutes on the tray.
