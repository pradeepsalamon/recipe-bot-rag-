# Lemon Rice (Elumichai Sadam)

`lemon_rice@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** PEANUT  
**May contain:** GLUTEN  
**Yield:** 3 servings  
**Times:** prep 5 min, cook 15 min

## Ingredients
- 450 g Cooked rice (cooled)
- 45 ml Lemon juice
- 40 g Roasted peanuts
- 10 g Chana dal
- 5 g Urad dal
- 3 g Mustard seeds
- 2 g Turmeric powder
- 1 g Asafoetida
- 2 pieces Green chili (slit)
- 1 sprig Curry leaves
- 20 g Oil
- 5 g Salt

## Method
1. Heat oil; add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
2. Add chana dal, urad dal and peanuts; fry until golden.
3. Add green chili, curry leaves, asafoetida and turmeric; stir 30 seconds.
4. Turn off the heat, add lemon juice and salt, then fold in the cooled rice.
5. Rest 5 minutes so the rice absorbs the tempering. Serve at room temperature.

## Notes
Leftover rice works best; freshly cooked rice turns mushy.
