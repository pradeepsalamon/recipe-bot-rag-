# Honey Oat Granola Bars (reformulated 2025)

`granola_bars@reformulated-2025` | status: **current**

> Supplier change 2025: butter replaced with tahini (sesame).

**Contains:** SESAME  
**May contain:** GLUTEN  
**Yield:** 12 bars  
**Times:** prep 10 min, cook 25 min

## Ingredients
- 250 g Rolled oats
- 120 g Honey
- 80 g Tahini
- 50 g Sunflower seeds
- 80 g Raisins
- 1 pinch Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 20 cm square tin.
2. Warm tahini and honey together; stir in oats, seeds, raisins and salt.
3. Press firmly into the tin and bake 25 minutes until golden at the edges.
4. Cool completely before cutting into 12 bars.
