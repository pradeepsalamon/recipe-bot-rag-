# Rasam

`rasam@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Times:** prep 5 min, cook 20 min

## Ingredients
- 50 g Toor dal (cooked, mashed)
- 30 g Tamarind pulp
- 100 g Tomato (chopped)
- 15 g Rasam powder
- 10 g Garlic (crushed)
- 5 g Mustard seeds
- 5 g Cumin seeds
- 1 sprig Curry leaves
- 15 g Ghee or oil
- 10 g Salt
- 1 L Water

## Method
1. Simmer tamarind pulp, tomatoes, rasam powder, garlic, and salt in water for 10-12 minutes.
2. Stir in the mashed toor dal and simmer gently (do not boil hard) for 5 minutes.
3. In a small pan, heat ghee and splutter mustard seeds, cumin, and curry leaves.
4. Pour the tempering over the rasam and remove from heat immediately.
5. Let it rest a few minutes for flavors to meld. Serve hot with steamed rice.

## Notes
Rasam should froth slightly when ready, a sign it has been simmered, not boiled.
