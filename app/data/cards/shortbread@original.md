# Butter Shortbread

`shortbread@original` | status: **superseded**

> SUPERSEDED by `shortbread@reformulated-2025`. Do not cook from this version.

**Contains:** GLUTEN, DAIRY  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 24 fingers  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 20 min

## Ingredients
- 300 g Plain flour
- 200 g Unsalted butter
- 100 g Caster sugar
- 50 g Cornflour
- 1 pinch Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 160°C. Cream the butter and sugar until pale.
2. Sift in flour, cornflour and salt; bring together without kneading.
3. Press into a 20 x 30 cm tin, prick all over and mark into 24 fingers.
4. Bake 18-20 minutes until pale golden; cut while warm and cool in the tin.
