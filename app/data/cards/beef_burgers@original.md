# Classic Beef Burgers

`beef_burgers@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** GLUTEN, EGG, FISH, SESAME  
**May contain:** SOY  
**Yield:** 4 burgers  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 10 min

## Ingredients
- 500 g Minced beef (20% fat)
- 30 g Breadcrumbs
- 1 piece Egg
- 10 ml Worcestershire sauce
- 4 pieces Sesame burger buns
- 5 g Salt
- 2 g Black pepper

## Method
1. Mix beef, breadcrumbs, egg, Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper; do not overwork.
2. Shape into 4 patties slightly wider than the buns; press a dimple in the centre.
3. Grill over high heat about 5 minutes per side until well browned.
4. Toast the buns and assemble.
