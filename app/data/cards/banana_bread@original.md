# Walnut Banana Bread

`banana_bread@original` | status: **superseded**

> SUPERSEDED by `banana_bread@reformulated-2025`. Do not cook from this version.

**Contains:** GLUTEN, DAIRY, EGG, TREE_NUT  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 1 loaf (10 slices)  
**Times:** prep 15 min, cook 60 min

## Ingredients
- 350 g Ripe bananas (mashed)
- 250 g Plain flour
- 100 g Unsalted butter (melted)
- 150 g Light brown sugar
- 2 pieces Eggs
- 75 g Walnuts (chopped)
- 5 g Bicarbonate of soda
- 2 g Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 175°C and line a 900 g loaf tin.
2. Mix bananas, melted butter, sugar and eggs.
3. Fold in flour, bicarbonate, salt and walnuts.
4. Bake about 60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean; cool 10 minutes in the tin.
