# Quick Peanut Noodles

`peanut_noodles@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** GLUTEN, PEANUT, SOY, SESAME  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 2 servings  
**Times:** prep 10 min, cook 10 min

## Ingredients
- 200 g Wheat noodles
- 60 g Peanut butter
- 30 ml Soy sauce
- 10 ml Toasted sesame oil
- 15 ml Lime juice
- 1 clove Garlic (grated)
- 2 pieces Spring onions

## Method
1. Cook the noodles according to the packet; reserve 60 ml of the water.
2. Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, sesame oil, lime and garlic with the reserved water.
3. Toss the noodles in the sauce and top with sliced spring onions.
