# Honey Oat Granola Bars

`granola_bars@original` | status: **superseded**

> SUPERSEDED by `granola_bars@reformulated-2025`. Do not cook from this version.

**Contains:** DAIRY  
**May contain:** GLUTEN  
**Yield:** 12 bars  
**Times:** prep 10 min, cook 25 min

## Ingredients
- 250 g Rolled oats
- 120 g Honey
- 80 g Unsalted butter
- 50 g Sunflower seeds
- 80 g Raisins
- 1 pinch Salt

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 170°C and line a 20 cm square tin.
2. Melt butter and honey together; stir in oats, seeds, raisins and salt.
3. Press firmly into the tin and bake 25 minutes until golden at the edges.
4. Cool completely before cutting into 12 bars.
