# Medu Vada

`medu_vada@original` | status: **current**

**Contains:** none declared  
**May contain:** none declared  
**Yield:** 12 vadas  
**Times:** prep 20 min, cook 25 min, rest/soak 240 min

## Ingredients
- 250 g Urad dal (split black gram)
- 10 g Green chili (chopped)
- 10 g Ginger (chopped)
- 5 g Black peppercorns
- 5 g Cumin seeds
- 5 g Curry leaves (chopped)
- 50 g Onion (finely chopped, optional)
- 10 g Salt
- Oil (for frying) (as needed)

## Method
1. Soak urad dal for 3-4 hours, then drain well.
2. Grind to a thick, fluffy batter using minimal water; the batter should hold its shape.
3. Fold in chilies, ginger, pepper, cumin, curry leaves, onion, and salt.
4. Wet hands, shape into small donuts with a hole in the center.
5. Deep fry in medium-hot oil, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crisp.
6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with sambar and coconut chutney.

## Notes
A stiff, well-aerated batter is key to a fluffy interior.
